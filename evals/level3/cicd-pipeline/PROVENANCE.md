## Declared patches (pre-applied CPP fixes)

Each is an exact replacement in BOTH copies of CPP's Python template - the
engine's embedded catalog in `scripts/project-init.py` and its mirror
`templates/project-init/python/pyproject.toml` - applied before the engine runs.

| Patch | Pre-applies | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `patches/cpp-1477.json` | cooneycw/claude-power-pack#1477 | the template's `build-backend = "hatchling.backends"` does not exist; unpatched, the engine stops at `uv sync` (Step 2) before git init |
| `patches/cpp-1478.json` | cooneycw/claude-power-pack#1478 | ruff/pytest sit in an optional `dev` extra that the generated `uv run` targets never install, and mypy is undeclared; a PEP 735 `[dependency-groups] dev` (with mypy) is what `uv run` installs by default |

`tests/test_level3_cicd_pipeline.py::test_the_declared_cpp_patches_still_apply_at_the_pin`
fails the day CPP fixes either issue at a new pin, with "CPP fixed #147x; drop
the patch deliberately" - the patches cannot silently outlive their reason.

`unpatched-real/` is the same flow WITHOUT the patches: the engine's real
`uv sync` failure is recorded (exit 1), and Step 4a and the pipeline step were
then run as a user continuing by hand would. It is the committed known-bad
control: a working grader must REJECT it (`pipeline-green` and
`behavior-preserved` VIOLATED).
