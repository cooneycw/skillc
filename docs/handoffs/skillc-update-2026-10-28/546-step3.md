## A. What the issue wants, in plain language

A report can currently call a scheduled slot "undecided" even though that slot never started. That makes a time-limited study look as though the agent ran out of time before choosing, when the study may simply have reached its overall limit before launching that slot. The selected change will count slots that did not run separately, keep genuinely inconclusive selection evidence visible, and leave the study's actual success rates and comparisons alone. It will not guess that missing evidence means no tokens were spent.
