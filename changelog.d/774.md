- **`make install-skill` writes a real copy of `/skill-gauntlet`, never a symlink** (#774). Kyle's deploy stages `~/.claude/skills` and fails on a symlink, which #763's link triggered. The copy carries a locator (`.skillc-checkout`, `~/`-relative under $HOME) and a marker (`.skillc-install`: kind, schema, digest; no paths). The launcher finds skillc through the locator. The copy is built in `${XDG_CACHE_HOME:-~/.cache}/skillc/install-tmp/` on the same device, so nothing temporary ever appears under the skills directory. Re-install replaces skillc's own copy, adopts a byte-identical unmarked copy, migrates #763's link, and refuses anything else. A stale copy is warned about and still runs. Uninstall removes only a marked copy.
