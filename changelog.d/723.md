- **`selftest` no longer certifies a semantic skill-usage rule on a control it cannot read** (#723). `skill-usage-fields`, `-privacy` and `-pairing` read only envelope-accepted lines, yet selftest counted any non-empty log as exercising them, so a good control whose every line had an unreadable envelope (`version: 2`, say) certified a rule that could refuse every real record. Such a log is now unexercised for those three rules, and either side refuses UNPARSED. The envelope rule keeps its own population.
