- **`skillc inspect`: the two #741 pass-3 false negatives fixed** (#745). A fully quoted
  option (`"--absent=x y"`) is checked against its owner like `--absent="x y"`, which
  fixes a regression against #731's code. An `env -S` / `--split-string` value is spliced
  back in as a new command, as env does, so the flags inside and after it are checked.
  Splices are capped at 32 per span and counted in `split_strings_not_examined`, never
  dropped silently.
