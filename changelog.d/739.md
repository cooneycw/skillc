- **`skillc contracts` no longer certifies mis-aligned list items or reads failed discovery as "none declared"** (#739). A `- key: value` item's keys must now line up under its first key, as YAML requires: the parser had assumed two columns right of the dash, so `-   id: x` with its keys left at the dash plus two was silently repaired and certified GOOD while PyYAML rejects it, and the aligned form was refused. A tab after a list dash is refused by name. Zero contracts beside a directory discovery could not read is now a `contract-shape` error, exit 1, not `CONTRACTS: none declared`, exit 3. New negative control: `controls/contract-shape/bad/dash-spacing`.
