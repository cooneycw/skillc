- **`skillc gauntlet verdict` and `skillc gauntlet rule`: skill-gauntlet round 5** (#737).
  `verdict` reads the gauntlet record, round 3/4 reports, the round 3/4
  declaration, `inspect` and overlap reports, and proposes exactly one of keep,
  trim or retire, or UNKNOWN naming the round that did not decide. It is never
  keep by default, and with no overlap report it reads UNKNOWN. It drafts a
  patch that only removes text, plus PR text, into the draft location, and never
  edits the skill or its charter. `rule` records the owner's accept, reject or
  amend in GAUNTLET.json, dated, and refuses a stale verdict digest or a skill or
  record that changed since the verdict. No model
  call is made.
