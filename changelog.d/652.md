- **Let captured executables run in the inventory oracle** (#652). Docker
  mounts `--tmpfs` with `noexec` unless told otherwise, so a captured `100755`
  helper staged under the oracle's `/tmp` failed with `EACCES` and a correct
  delivery was graded as breaking legacy behaviour. The oracle's `/tmp` now
  mounts with `exec` (`nosuid` and `nodev` kept), the launch argv unit test
  pins it, and the inventory builder's dependency digest is re-pinned.
