- **The authority interceptor no longer blames a clean subject for the backend's own half-written `observations`** (#761). `DockerBackend.execute()` now declares its write-back (`ExecuteResult.observations_write`: its monotonic window and the exact bytes). `AuthorityInterceptor.stop_and_finalize(write_back=...)` drops an `observations` transition only when that write explains it: the poll could have seen the write, the value was absent or a byte prefix of what was written, and the settled value is the write's own. A subject's own create (an empty file included), its delete, and a left-running child's overwrite after the write-back are all still flagged. Without `write_back`, the old rule is unchanged. `scripts/probe_docker_cp_writeback.py` measures what a real daemon's `docker cp` shows a concurrent reader.
  The timing ratchet (#758) gains a second annotation kind, `# timing: cadence - <condition and deadline>`, for a poll interval inside a loop that waits on a condition with its own deadline (orchestrator ruling); `must-fire` and `cadence` are the whole vocabulary, and #761's held-window tests annotate their poll cadences this way.
