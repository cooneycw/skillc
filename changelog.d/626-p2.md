- **`ProtocolWave` can hand its measured turns to the wake driver** (#626 P2). `measured="wake"` runs `wave_wake.drive` with the caller's approved policy, keeping the protocol order. Three keyword-only `drive` hooks make that possible, each inert at its default: `after_bootstrap` (bind, barrier, freeze and serve after every bootstrap and before any poke), `before_close` (fence and take the live census before `wave.close()`), and `defer_terminal_stop` (a terminal role stays running, with no further turn or poke, until close). The wake capture's own context digest joins `protocol_v2`'s clock join, and the report carries the `wake` binding. Wake mode refuses a journal declaration or deadline that differs from the wake capture's. The scripted path is unchanged.
